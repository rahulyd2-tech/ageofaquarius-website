Platform · 01 · Digital Twin

See every site before, during and after the work.

Survey drawings, drone imagery and field records come together in one live 3D model of the land, which we keep updating from the first survey to handover.

Built with
Before

Know the land

Drones by
  • 3D terrain built from the surveyor’s CAD file and drone imagery
  • Legal boundaries shown as fences, with every tree mapped
  • Existing dams, embankments and tracks placed on the model
  • Slope, sun-facing and cut-and-fill figures for planning
During

Track the work

  • Engineers log fencing, roads, pipelines, wells and excavation at exact GPS points
  • Geo-tagged photos, videos and drone flight paths on the same map
  • Site video matched to the 3D view, so you can stand where it was filmed
After

Hand over a record

  • Every road, pipe, well and fence mapped where it was actually built
  • Photo and video proof tied to each item
  • Maps and data exported for owners, buyers and approvals
